Question: How do I update my personal details like my address or bank account?
Answer: Log into the internal employee self-service portal, go to your profile settings, and update your banking or residential information under the "Personal Info" tab. For payroll bank changes, submit a voided check to HR.

Question: What is our company policy on remote and hybrid work?
Answer: Employees are allowed up to two days of remote work per week, subject to manager approval and team scheduling needs. Core working hours must still be observed regardless of location.

Question: How many paid time off (PTO) days do I get each year?
Answer: Full-time employees receive 20 days of PTO annually, accrued on a monthly basis. Unused days roll over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year.

Question: What health insurance benefits are available to me?
Answer: The company offers comprehensive medical, dental, and vision coverage through our primary provider. You can enroll or modify your dependents during the annual open enrollment period every November.

Question: How do I request approval for professional development courses?
Answer: Fill out the educational assistance form available on the HR intranet and get sign-off from your direct supervisor. The company reimburses up to $1,500 per year for approved career-related certifications or courses.

Question: What should I do if I experience or witness workplace harassment?
Answer: Report the incident immediately to the HR grievance committee or use our anonymous ethics hotline. All reports are investigated confidentially with a strict zero-tolerance policy for retaliation.

Question: How does the annual performance review process work?
Answer: Performance reviews occur bi-annually in June and December. Employees complete a self-evaluation, followed by a review meeting and goal-setting session with their manager.

Question: What is the process for taking parental leave?
Answer: Eligible employees receive up to 12 weeks of paid parental leave following the birth or adoption of a child. Notify HR at least 30 days in advance with the expected timeline to coordinate coverage.

Question: Where can I find our company safety and emergency guidelines?
Answer: Detailed emergency evacuation plans and safety protocols are posted on every floor near the elevators and on the company handbook page. Safety wardens conduct bi-annual fire drills for all staff.

Question: Who do I contact for payroll discrepancies or tax withholding questions?
Answer: Reach out directly to the payroll division via the internal ticketing system or email [payroll@company.com](mailto:payroll@company.com). Changes to state or federal tax withholdings can be updated directly via the payroll portal.
